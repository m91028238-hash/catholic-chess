Checkmate in the Sistine

An engraved wooden board, ivory and oxblood pieces, inside a 3D Sistine Chapel painted with Michelangelo’s ceiling and Last Judgement and the 15th-century wall frescoes.

Each game starts with you standing in the chapel: walk around, use the Artworks buttons and zoom to see the paintings up close, use fullscreen (⛶) or turn your phone sideways, then walk to your chair to sit and play.

How to play

Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a dot to move. Pinch to zoom; the “Camera” button cycles Player, Top, Side, Walk and Free cam (Free cam circles the board steadily: drag to orbit, pinch, the wheel or + and − to move in close, two fingers or right-drag to slide; tapping still plays moves, and the Artworks strip glides to a straight-on view of any fresco. Walk lets you stroll the chapel as a pilgrim: the ring at the bottom left walks, dragging anywhere else turns your head all the way round and up to Michelangelo’s ceiling, and pinching or the + and − buttons zoom in on detail; on a computer, WASD or the arrows walk, Shift walks faster, drag to look and the wheel zooms. The Artworks strip walks you to a good spot in front of any fresco, or under the ceiling looking up; Back to board returns you to your seat). Win a campaign game to unlock the next opponent; you can replay any unlocked level.
