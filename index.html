Catholic Chess

An engraved wooden board, ivory and oxblood pieces, inside real 360° photographs of St Peter’s Basilica and other holy places.

Mode

Difficulty

Campaign ladder

Play as

Turn the board to the side to move

Setting

Clock (each side)

How to play

Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a dot to move. Pinch to zoom; the “Camera” button cycles Player, Top, Side and Free views (in Free, drag to rotate, two-finger drag or right-drag to pan, pinch, wheel or the + and − buttons to zoom; drag up to look all the way up at the vault and dome). Win a campaign game to unlock the next opponent; you can replay any unlocked level.
