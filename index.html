Catholic Chess

An engraved wooden board, ivory and oxblood pieces, inside a 3D Sistine Chapel painted with Michelangelo’s ceiling and Last Judgement and the 15th-century wall frescoes.

Mode

Difficulty

Campaign ladder

Play as

Turn the board to the side to move

Clock (each side)

How to play

Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a dot to move. Pinch to zoom; the “Camera” button cycles Player, Top, Side and Free views (in Free, drag to rotate, two-finger drag or right-drag to pan, pinch, wheel or the + and − buttons to zoom; drag up to look all the way up at Michelangelo’s ceiling, and turn around to see every wall). Win a campaign game to unlock the next opponent; you can replay any unlocked level.
