Catholic Chess

An engraved wooden board, boxwood and rosewood pieces.

Mode

Difficulty

Campaign ladder

Turn the board to the side to move

Clock (each side)

How to play

Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a dot to move. Pinch to zoom; the “Camera” button cycles Player, Top and Side views. Win a campaign game to unlock the next opponent; you can replay any unlocked level.
